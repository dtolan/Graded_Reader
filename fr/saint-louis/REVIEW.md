Issues in the text:
- **low** “Il y est arrivé en mille deux cent cinquante (1250)” — Louis arrived at Acre on 13 May 1250, shortly after his release, so this is fine. However, the chapter does not mention that he first spent time at Acre before the other prisoners were all freed.
- **low** “Il avait perdu son frère.” — Robert d'Artois died at Mansourah, but his other brothers Alphonse de Poitiers and Charles d'Anjou were also captured or present. The statement is true but could mislead by implying only one brother was involved.
- **low** “Bouleversés par la captivité du roi, ils voulaient, disaient-ils, aller le délivrer.” — The movement started in 1251 under the Master of Hungary, who claimed a mission from the Virgin. The chapter omits the violence against clergy and others that prompted Blanche's action, so it presents a simplified picture.
- **low** “Après quatre années passées en Orient, dont une partie dans la captivité et le reste à relever des murailles” — Louis' captivity lasted only about a month, so describing the four years as partly captivity is misleading.
- **low** “Son armée a été libérée avec lui, du moins ceux qui avaient survécu aux combats et à la captivité.” — Many ordinary prisoners were not freed with the king, and some were massacred; they were released more slowly after the first payment.
- **low** “Il a été décidé que la moitié de cette somme serait versée avant la libération du souverain.” — The king's own release was tied to payment of the first half, but Damietta's surrender also formed part of the terms for his freedom; the order of events is slightly simplified.
